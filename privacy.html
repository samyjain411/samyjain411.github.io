1. Introduction

Gochar Orrery ("the App", "we", "our") is a Vedic astronomical calendar and educational visualisation tool developed by Unalome Dev. This Privacy Policy describes what information the App collects, how it is used, how it is stored, and your rights regarding that information.

By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App and do not use it.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your birth details to any external server.
  • We do not build behavioural profiles or sell any data to third parties.
  • We do not use any analytics SDK (no Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude).
  • We do not collect payment card details. All subscription billing is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play billing systems. We receive only a confirmation of purchase status, not payment details.
  • We do not use cookies (the App is a native mobile application, not a web service).

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy which governs how they handle data.

5.1 Firebase Authentication (Google LLC)

Used for Google Sign-In. Your Google account name and email address are transmitted to Firebase when you sign in. Firebase Authentication privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5.2 Google AdMob (Google LLC)

Used to serve rewarded video advertisements in the Playground feature. AdMob collects your advertising identifier and standard ad interaction events. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time via your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Personalised Ads (off)

AdMob privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5.3 Astronomical Calculation Library

The astronomical calculation library runs entirely on-device. It does not transmit any data to external servers.

7. Data Security

Your birth details and notification preferences are stored locally on your device using the standard Android and iOS secure preferences mechanism. They are not transmitted to our servers. Firebase Authentication uses industry-standard TLS encryption for all network communication.

We take reasonable technical precautions to protect your information; however, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at unalome.dev@gmail.com and we will delete the information promptly.
