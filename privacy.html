Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026  ·  Developer: Unalome Dev  ·  unalome.dev@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Gochar ("the App", "we", "our") is a Vedic calendar and sky visualisation app developed by Unalome Dev. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not show advertisements, and the App does not include an advertising or analytics SDK.
  • We do not build behavioural profiles of what you do in the App.
  • We do not collect payment details. The App does not currently sell anything; if purchases are added, they will be handled by Google Play or the App Store.

5. How your information is used

We use the information above only to run the App's features: calculating your Panchang and charts, keeping your profile in sync across devices, and sending the notifications you have turned on. Daily content, such as the day's reading, stories and mantras, is the same for everyone and is fetched from our servers; your personal details are not used to generate it.

6. Third-party services

The App relies on the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy.

6.1 Google Firebase

Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions and Remote Config, provided by Google LLC. Data is sent over encrypted connections. firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6.2 Sign-in providers

If you sign in with Google or Apple, that provider shares your name and email address with the App as part of sign-in. If you use Sign in with Apple, the authorisation is revoked when you delete your account. Google privacy policy  ·  Apple privacy policy

6.3 Links to other apps

Some content, such as music for a raga, opens in another service like YouTube. Once you leave the App, that service's own privacy policy applies.

6.4 Calculations

All astronomical calculations run on your device. No data is sent anywhere to calculate them.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the App at Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your sign-in account, your account record in Cloud Firestore, and the data stored on your device. If you can no longer open the App, see Delete your account.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to your account record is restricted to your signed-in account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact unalome.dev@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India) and other applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data (you can edit your birth details in the App at any time)
  • Erase your personal data
  • Withdraw consent, for example by turning off location or notifications in your device settings

To exercise these rights, email unalome.dev@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
