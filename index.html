02 — The orrery

The positions behind the Panchang.

Earth at the centre, east on the left, west on the right. The Sun moves across the top during the day, and the Moon and each planet sit on their own ring.

Orrery

Tithi is an angle

The gap between Sun and Moon, seen from Earth. Every 12° is one tithi; thirty make the month.

Muhurtas follow sunrise

Daylight in fifteen parts from your sunrise, the night in fifteen more. The current one is highlighted.

Retrogrades leave a loop

Each planet trails its recent path. When the trail folds back, the planet is retrograde.
