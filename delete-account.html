Developer: Unalome Dev  ·  unalome.dev@gmail.com

Delete it in the app

The quickest way is from inside Gochar. Deletion happens straight away.

  1. Open Gochar and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Settings, then Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in again before the account is removed.

Can't open the app?

Email unalome.dev@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request. Send it from the email address you sign in with, or include the phone number you sign in with, so we can find your account. We will delete it within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account, so you can no longer sign in with it. If you used Sign in with Apple, the App's authorisation with Apple is revoked too.
  • Your account record on our servers: your name, birth details and saved location.
  • Data on your device (when you delete in the app): your profile, the extra chart profiles in My Charts, preferences and cached data.

The notification registration for your device can also be removed on request: mention it in your email. Notification history is removed automatically after a few days.

Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted it cannot be recovered, and your birth details and chart profiles will need to be entered again if you sign up later.
